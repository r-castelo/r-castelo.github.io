Inner Meridian privacy policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Inner Meridian is a meditation app made by Renato Castelo Branco ("I", "me"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app on Android or iOS.

The short version

Inner Meridian doesn't collect your personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. What you do in the app stays on your phone.

What the app stores, and where

The app keeps these on your device only, in its private storage:

  • your practice history (which sessions you've played, when, and for how long);
  • the mood you choose in the check-in before a session;
  • your journal entries;
  • your session feedback notes, if you write any;
  • your settings, such as reminders, sounds and volumes.

None of this is sent to me or to anyone else. I can't see it, and I can't recover it if you lose your phone.

When information leaves your phone

Only when you choose to send it:

  • Backups. Settings → Back up and restore creates a file and opens your phone's share sheet. Where it goes (your files, a cloud drive, an email) is your choice and is covered by that service's own privacy terms.
  • Feedback. Settings → Session feedback → Export creates a file with your notes, and "Email" buttons open your email app with a note addressed to me. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself. If you send it, I use it only to improve the sessions, and I delete it when I no longer need it. Ask and I'll delete it sooner.

Purchases

If you buy the full Atlas, the payment is handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their privacy policies. I don't receive your payment details. The app checks on your phone that the purchase is valid.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, the app schedules them on your phone. No notification service or server is involved.

Children

Inner Meridian is meant for adults and isn't directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). It doesn't knowingly collect any data from anyone, children included.

Deleting your data

Settings → Reset progress deletes your practice history and journal. Settings → Session feedback has its own button to delete your feedback notes. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored. Backup files you exported stay wherever you saved them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date. If the app ever starts collecting data, this policy and the store listings will say so before that version is released.
