Inner Meridian terms of purchase

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Inner Meridian is made by Renato Castelo Branco ("I", "me"). These terms cover the one-time purchase that unlocks the full Atlas in the app.

The short version

You pay once and keep what you bought. It's like buying an album: you get the Atlas as it is on the day you buy it, to play as often as you like. You aren't buying a subscription, future content or ongoing support.

What's free

Without paying you can play the first three sessions of Basecamp, the first session of every other journey, the breathing exercise and the timer. Nothing free expires.

What the purchase includes

The full Atlas: all 10 journeys (Basecamp and 9 more) with 100 guided sessions, plus 27 singles, which is every session in the app on the day you buy. The app's store page and the unlock screen list them.

The purchase stays with your Google Play account (or Apple ID on iOS). If you reinstall the app or move to a new phone with the same account, tap Restore purchase in Settings. Sessions play offline once they're on your phone.

What it doesn't include

  • Future content. If I add journeys, sessions or features later, they may or may not be included in your purchase. I'm not promising any.
  • A subscription or ongoing service. There is no account and no server. The app runs on your phone.
  • Support or updates for a set period. I may update the app, but I don't promise to keep updating it or answer support requests on a schedule.

None of this limits the rights you have under the consumer law where you live. For example, the app has to work as described when you buy it.

Price and payment

Google Play or the Apple App Store takes the payment, in your local currency, and handles taxes. The price is shown on the unlock screen before you pay.

Refunds

Refunds go through the store you bought from. On Google Play, see Google's refund policy or use Order history in the Play Store. On iOS, use Apple's "Report a problem". If you get a refund, the full Atlas locks again. Your practice history and journal stay on your phone.

Promo codes

A Google Play promo code unlocks the same full Atlas as a purchase, under the same terms.

Not medical care

Inner Meridian is for meditation practice. It isn't therapy or medical treatment, and it doesn't replace either.
