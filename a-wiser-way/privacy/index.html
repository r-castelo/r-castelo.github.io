A Wiser Way privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

A Wiser Way is an app of guided audio sessions about philosophy, made by Renato Castelo Branco ("I", "me"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

The short version

Nothing leaves your phone unless you send it yourself. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What stays on your phone

The app keeps your progress along the road, where you stopped in a session, your journal entries and unfinished writing, and your settings. All of this is stored only on your device. I don't run a server, and I can't see any of it.

Backups

If you make a backup, the app creates a file and opens your phone's share sheet. Where that file goes is your choice: your files, a cloud drive, an email to yourself. I never receive it. Anyone who has the file can read your journal, so keep it somewhere private.

Reminders

If you turn on the monthly backup reminder, the app schedules a notification on your phone. Android may ask you to allow notifications. Nothing is sent to me.

Purchases

The one-time purchase is handled by Google Play under Google's own privacy policy. The app checks on your phone that the purchase was made; I receive no payment card or personal details from it. Google may share basic sales information with developers, such as the country of a purchase.

Tester feedback

Test versions have a feedback button. Your notes stay on your phone until you share them yourself, through your phone's share sheet.

Deleting your information

"Start over" in the app's menu erases your progress, journal and unfinished writing on the phone. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored. Backup files you saved elsewhere stay until you delete them.

Children

A Wiser Way is made for adults and isn't directed at children.

Changes

If the app ever starts collecting information, this policy will change first, and the new version will be posted here with a new date.
